      <div class="pkj-project-summary">
        <p>A digital strategy is only useful if it can be read and acted on by the people making business decisions. This project wrote a structured digital strategy roadmap for Pro Clean Enterprises: framed the business problem, proposed a phased set of technology initiatives, and thought through adoption sequencing, constraints, and value/risk tradeoffs.</p>
        <p>The final document was designed to work for two different readers: a technical stakeholder who would evaluate feasibility, and a business decision-maker who needed clear recommendations they could act on. Getting that dual readability right was the central challenge. No pictures for this one, since the deliverable was a written strategy document rather than a data visualization or technical build.</p>
