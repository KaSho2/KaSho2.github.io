      <div class="pkj-project-summary">
        <p>Workstation 13 at the Doosan Bobcat ZT2 assembly line had a tire installation problem. Workers were walking more steps than necessary, tools were not where hands needed to go, postures were awkward, and cycle times were inconsistent from operator to operator.</p>
        <p>Mapped the current state with SIPOC and a spaghetti diagram. Went through a PDCA cycle to isolate root causes and build targeted recommendations: 5S organization, ergonomic lifting fixtures, and standard work documentation so the improvements would hold.</p>
        <p>MOST analysis gave a quantified cycle-time improvement estimate. Built the cost-benefit case with a sustainability plan including audit schedules and training requirements.</p>
